01 / UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which criminals impersonate trusted people or organizations to trick victims into revealing sensitive information or taking unsafe actions.

📧

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails pretend to come from trusted companies and may contain suspicious links or unexpected attachments.

EMAIL-BASED
🎯

Spear Phishing

Targeted messages use personal or professional details to make a scam appear more believable.

TARGETED ATTACK
📱

Smishing

Phishing through SMS or text messages, often involving fake delivery notices or account alerts.

SMS-BASED
☎️

Vishing

Voice calls are used to impersonate banks, technical support, or other trusted organizations.

VOICE-BASED

03 / RECOGNIZE THE RED FLAGS

Warning signs you shouldn't ignore

Phishing messages often use psychological pressure to make people act before checking the facts.

01

Urgent Threats

Messages claim your account will be blocked unless you act immediately.

02

Suspicious Links

A link may lead to an unrelated or misspelled domain instead of the expected website.

03

Unexpected Requests

A sender requests passwords, verification codes, payment details, or confidential data.

04

Unusual Attachments

Unexpected files may contain malicious content or lead to unsafe downloads.

04 / PROTECT YOURSELF

Build your security habits

A few careful decisions can significantly reduce the risk of falling for phishing.

🔗

Inspect Links

Check the destination domain before opening a link. Navigate to important services using their official app or website.

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for different accounts and consider a trusted password manager.

🛡️

Enable MFA

Use multi-factor authentication to add another layer of account protection.

📨

Verify the Sender

Confirm unexpected requests through a separate, trusted communication channel.

🔄

Update Your Devices

Keep your operating system, browser, and security software updated.

🚨

Report Suspicious Messages

Use your email provider's phishing-reporting feature or contact your organization's IT team.
